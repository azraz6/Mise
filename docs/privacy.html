Privacy Policy

A recipe box should keep your recipes, not collect your life. Mise runs on your iPhone, without an account.

Last updated: October 31, 2026

The short version

  • Mise has no account, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no developer-operated server.
  • Your recipes, notes, photos, collections, shopping lists, meal plans, and import history stay on your iPhone.
  • Mise only goes online when you ask it to read a public recipe page or download a cover image.
  • Screenshot text recognition runs on your device. Your photos are not uploaded.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. Mise never sees your payment details.

What Mise stores on your device

Mise stores the content you create or import in its own on-device database, including:

  • Recipe titles, ingredients, steps, notes, tags, and source links.
  • Cover photos you choose and images you import.
  • Collections, shopping lists, meal plans, favourites, and cooking progress.
  • Import history, including the address you pasted and any error returned by a site.
  • A local record of your free import allowance and your subscription entitlement.

This information stays in Mise's application container on your device. It is not sent to the developer, and Mise does not sync it to iCloud or any other service.

Network requests

Mise makes a network request only when you start an automatic import from a link or when a recipe includes a cover image that Mise needs to download.

  • Mise requests the public web page you supplied, using ordinary HTTPS.
  • The website you visit can see standard technical information such as your IP address and user agent, just as it would if you opened the page in a browser. The developer does not receive or retain that information.
  • Mise does not sign in to websites, bypass paywalls, CAPTCHAs, or access controls, scrape private content, or download video from social platforms.
  • If a page cannot be read, Mise saves the link or asks you to paste text, use a screenshot, or enter the recipe yourself.

Photos and screenshots

When you choose screenshots or photos, Mise uses Apple's system photo picker. Text recognition is performed on your device with Apple's Vision framework. The images and recognized text are used to build a draft recipe and are stored locally. They are not uploaded to the developer.

Share extension

When you share a link, text, or image to Mise from another app, the shared item is written to a private App Group container on your device. Mise reads that item the next time you open the app. Shared content is not sent to a server.

Purchases and subscriptions

Mise Premium is sold through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes the purchase and manages your subscription. Mise receives a verified entitlement that tells the app whether Premium is active, along with the product identifier and expiration date. Mise does not receive your name, payment card, or billing address.

Data retention and deletion

  • You can erase every recipe, collection, shopping list, meal plan entry, and import record from Settings → Erase all data.
  • Deleting the Mise app removes its on-device data, subject to your device's normal backup behavior.
  • Because Mise has no account or server copy, the developer cannot recover your library for you. Keep your own backups if the content matters to you.
  • Subscription records are retained by Apple according to Apple's policies. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings.

Children

Mise is a general cooking tool and is not directed to children under 13. Mise does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Third-party services

Mise relies on Apple frameworks for the App Store, photo picking, text recognition, and web requests. Opening a privacy, support, or recipe link takes you to a website operated by someone else, and that site's privacy policy applies. Mise includes starter photography from Unsplash under the Unsplash License; those images are bundled with the app.

Changes to this policy

If Mise's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date above will change. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
