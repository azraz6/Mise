Last updated: October 31, 2026

Contact us

Email info@qshoppro.com with the subject “Mise support”. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were trying to do. We usually reply within two business days.

Importing a recipe

Tap Save a recipe on Home, then choose one of four methods:

  • Link — paste a public recipe page address. Mise first looks for published recipe data, then readable page text.
  • Text — paste a caption, message, or recipe text. Extraction happens on your iPhone.
  • Photos — choose up to four screenshots or photos. Text recognition runs on device.
  • Write it yourself — always free and unlimited.

Every import opens a review screen before anything is saved. Check quantities, times, and steps against the original.

What Mise can and cannot import

  • Recipe websites: best results when the site publishes structured recipe data. Otherwise Mise reads visible page text and asks you to review it.
  • Instagram, TikTok, Facebook: Mise does not sign in, scrape private posts, or download video. Save the link, then paste the caption or use a screenshot.
  • YouTube and Pinterest: public metadata may be available, but the full recipe often lives in a description, video, or linked page. Add what is missing yourself.
  • Paywalls, logins, CAPTCHAs: Mise never bypasses access controls. Save the link and add the recipe from text you have access to.
  • Screenshots: on-device recognition can misread fractions and small type. Compare the draft with the original.

Free plan and Mise Premium

Mise is free for manual recipes, cooking mode, shopping lists, meal planning, search, and your whole library. The free plan includes 20 automatic imports per calendar month and up to 3 collections. Mise Premium removes those two limits.

Premium is available as a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription. Prices are shown in the app before you confirm and are charged to your Apple Account. Manage or cancel in Apple's subscription settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not refund the current period.

If Premium does not appear after purchase, open Settings → Restore purchases. If it still does not appear, contact us with the date of purchase and the Apple Account country, but never send payment details.

Your library and backups

Everything is stored on this iPhone. There is no account and no cloud sync. Deleting the app or using Settings → Erase all data removes your recipes permanently. The developer cannot recover them.

If your device backs up to iCloud or a computer, the app's container may be included according to your backup settings. Mise does not control or guarantee those backups.

Cooking mode and timers

Cooking mode keeps the screen awake while it is open. Step timers run only while Mise is open; Mise does not schedule background notifications. If you leave the app, the timer will not alert you.

Shopping lists

Mise merges amounts only when the ingredient name and unit match exactly. Different units, preparations, or spellings stay as separate lines so nothing disappears at the store. You can edit any item, check it off, or move it between lists.

Accessibility

Mise supports Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels, reduced motion, and light and dark appearance. If a screen is hard to use with your settings, email us with the screen name and your accessibility configuration.

Troubleshooting

  • “That link needs a check”: use a full public https:// address. Local, private, and sign-in-only addresses are rejected.
  • Import limit reached: manual recipes stay unlimited. The automatic allowance resets at the start of each calendar month.
  • A page imports poorly: try the Text or Photos tab, or write the recipe yourself. Review every draft before saving.
  • Subscription unavailable: check your connection and try again. On a new device, use Restore purchases.
